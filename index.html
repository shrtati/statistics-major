Analytics & Biostats Career Explorer Plan

3-Month Hands-On Sandbox to Test Your Fit • From Scratch to Master-Ready

📊 Path 1: Applied Statistics & Business Analytics

Goal: Turn messy transactional logs into visual executive decisions.

Month 1: SQL Foundations & Python Data Wrangling
  • SQL Segmenting Exercise
    Extract users with purchases in the last 30 days using basic aggregation.
    SELECT CustomerID, SUM(UnitPrice*Quantity) AS Spend
    FROM Transactions GROUP BY CustomerID HAVING Spend > 0;
    🔗 Resource: Google Advanced Data Analytics (Course 2 & 3)
    "Alumni Note: In real life, 60% of your time is spent in SQL cleaning transactional logs before doing any fancy analytics."
  • Load Open-Source Dataset
    Download the UCI Online Retail Dataset (or Kaggle Advertising Dataset for a simplified start). Set up your Python environment using Pandas.
    🔗 Download Dataset: UCI Online Retail
Month 2: Predictive Modeling (Regression)
  • Run Applied Regression
    In Python or R, regress total spend against frequency and average basket size to determine key purchase drivers.
    🔗 Course: Business Statistics & Analysis Specialization (Rice Univ) 🔗 Video: Basic Statistical Functions in R
    "Alumni Note: Executives don't care about your $R^2$ values. They want to know where to direct the marketing budget."
Month 3: Dashboards & Non-Technical Delivery
  • Build a Visual Dashboard
    Connect your cleaned dataset to Power BI or Tableau to build an interactive dashboard tracking high-value customer cohorts.
  • 5-Slide Business Pitch Deliverable
    Prepare a 5-slide presentation. Challenge: Explain your findings without using words like "regression" or "p-value".
🎁 Path 1 Milestone Reward: Treat yourself to a premium coffee or lunch when you complete Month 1, and buy a book on visualization design (e.g., Storytelling with Data) when you complete the 5-slide deck!

🩺 Path 2: Biostatistics & Clinical Research

Goal: Measure safety, survival rates, and drug efficacy with scientific precision.

Month 1: Clinical Foundations & Vectors
  • R/SAS Fundamentals & Data Loading
    Learn basic vector manipulation in R/SAS and import clinical cohort records.
    🔗 Video: Basic Statistical Functions in R 🔗 Download Dataset: UCI Heart Failure Clinical Records
    "Alumni Note: Biostats requires high precision. Standardizing your cohort definitions early saves months of regulatory headache."
Month 2: Survival Analysis & Hypothesis Testing
  • Kaplan-Meier Curve Construction
    Plot survival probability using the time and DEATH_EVENT features. Compare high blood pressure vs. normal pressure groups.
    🔗 Course: Improving Your Statistical Inferences (TU Eindhoven)
  • Log-Rank Rigor Check
    Perform a log-rank test to verify if the survival differences between patient cohorts are statistically significant.
    "Alumni Note: In pharma, standard statistical assumptions are non-negotiable. Replicability is everything."
Month 3: Clinical Reporting & Industry Reflection
  • 1-Page Clinical Study Report
    Write a formal summary documenting sample sizing, hazard ratios, and regulatory compliance standards for your treatment trial.
  • Industry Trade-Off Reflection
    Evaluate job security vs. specialized domain constraints by engaging with domain discussions.
    🔗 Industry Discussion: r/statistics Biostats vs Stats Debate
🎁 Path 2 Milestone Reward: Order your favorite take-out after completing your first Kaplan-Meier curve, and treat yourself to a day off from coding after completing the Clinical Report!
